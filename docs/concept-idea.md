Management Software for Small Property Owners

## 1. Niche

**Individuals (PF) or Legal Entities (PJ)** owning **1 to 10 rental properties** and managing contracts directly, without real estate agencies. Typically retirees, beginner investors, or professionals who bought properties as a store of value.

## 2. Problem

Managing rentals requires tracking: bill due dates, annual adjustments (IGPM/INPC), late fees, move-in/move-out inspections, maintenance coordination, and tenant communication. Owners manage this using **spreadsheets, notebooks, or memory**, resulting in:

- **Missed adjustments** (real revenue loss).
- **Late rent not collected** (silent delinquency).
- **Lost documents** (contracts, inspection photos).
- **Stressful communication** (WhatsApp mixed with personal conversations).

## 3. Current Solution

- **Excel/Google Sheets:** For tracking due dates and adjustment calculations.
- **Phone Calendar:** For payment reminders.
- **WhatsApp:** For communication with tenants and maintenance providers.
- **Paper/PDF Contracts:** Stored in physical folders or on Drive with no organization.

## 4. Existing Competition

- **Alugo (R$ 29.90/month):** Very simple, focused only on digital contracts but lacks collection automation and adjustments.
- **Superlógica / QuintoAndar (management):** Extremely comprehensive but geared towards **real estate agencies and large administrators** (high price and overkill for 1-10 properties).
- **GeraContratos:** Only document generation, no delinquency management.
- **Indirect Competition:** Spreadsheets and manual reminders.

**Why There's Room:** There's a "valley" in the market. Alugo is too shallow; Superlógica is too complex/expensive. There's a gap for an intermediate product that automates **collections** and **adjustments** for small investors who don't want to pay 10% commission to a real estate agency.

## 5. Proposed SaaS

**Code Name:** _Alugasim_
Centralized dashboard showing all properties, tenants, and payment status. Features:

- Contract registration with adjustment data (index and base date).
- **Automatic adjustment calculation** and generation of new bills/Pix charges.
- Automatic email/WhatsApp reminders for tenants (3 days before due date).
- Delinquency tracking (days overdue, sending collection notices).
- Inspection photo gallery attached to each property.
- Maintenance history (expenses).

## 6. Why Customers Would Pay

- **Revenue Recovery:** Applying IGPM adjustments (which can exceed 10% annually) prevents inflation erosion. On a R$ 2,000/month property, that's R$ 200/year saved per property.
- **Reduced Delinquency:** The system automatically charges, preventing tenant "forgetfulness" and owner embarrassment in collecting.
- **Time Savings:** Owners stop spending 3 hours/month organizing spreadsheets.

## 7. Pricing

**R$ 50 to R$ 79/month** (lower ticket because more customers are needed, but the market is enormous).

## 8. Path to BRL 5,000 MRR

- Average price R$ 70/month → **72 customers**.
- There are millions of property owners in Brazil (over 5 million rental properties). Finding 72 willing to pay for automation is highly feasible.

## 9. Customer Acquisition

1. **Facebook and Instagram Ads:** Target "investment interests," "retirees," and location (upscale neighborhoods).
2. **WhatsApp/Facebook Groups:** "Property Owners" and "Individual Investor" groups.
3. **Real Estate Agent Partnerships:** Offer commissions for agents who refer clients who bought properties for investment.
4. **Google Maps/Street View:** Search for properties with "For Rent" signs and call the phone number directly.

## 10. Market Accessibility: 9/10

Owners are massively accessible via paid ads (Facebook/Instagram) as they are regular internet users. The challenge is conversion.

## 11. Competition: 6/10

Alugo has a low price but doesn't solve the main pain (collection and adjustment). Superlógica is inaccessible. The market is large enough for another player.

## 12. Pain Intensity: 6/10

The pain is financial, but many owners are complacent and only notice the loss at year-end. It's not acute pain (like an inspection) but chronic pain.

## 13. Willingness to Pay: 6/10

The average Brazilian resists paying for SaaS (preferring "free"). It needs to prove ROI very clearly on the landing page (e.g., "You're losing R$ 500/year by not applying adjustments").

## 14. MVP Difficulty: 3/10

Technically trivial: CRUD for properties, contracts, interest/adjustment calculations (simple math formulas), email/WhatsApp sending.

## 15. Overall Opportunity Score: **62/100**

Enormous market but low ticket and challenging payment culture. Requires high volume (72+ customers) and strong digital marketing.

### Top 3 Analysis Summary

- **Exact ICP:** Individual investor with 2-5 properties, aged 40-65, who already uses internet banking and seeks to optimize investments.
- **Core Problem:** "I always forget to adjust the rent and feel embarrassed to ask the tenant."
- **MVP Feature Set:** Contract registration, adjustment calculator, email due date alerts.
- **What NOT to Build:** Notary integration for contract registration, complex financial modules (income tax).
- **Biggest Risk:** Customers may think R$ 70/month is expensive because "Excel does that."
- **Validation (30 days):** Create an automated spreadsheet (Google Sheets) that calculates adjustments and offer it for free in exchange for an email. Downloaders are qualified leads. Sell the SaaS to these leads.
